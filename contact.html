Good to know

Frequently asked
questions.

How do I book a room?

The fastest way is WhatsApp — tap any “Book” button on this site and send us your dates, number of guests and room type. You can also call us on +977 986-1992777.

How quickly will you reply?

We reply as soon as our mountain connection allows — usually the same day. In bad weather the internet can be slow, so thank you for your patience.

What are your room rates?

Rates depend on the season, room type and group size. Message us your dates and we’ll send you today’s price.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes — Wi-Fi is free for guests. Speeds can vary with the weather at this altitude.

Can you cater for vegetarian or other diets?

Of course. Let us know your dietary needs when you book and our kitchen will look after you.

Do you host trekking groups?

Yes, we regularly welcome guided groups, guides and porters. Message us early in the peak seasons so we can reserve enough rooms.

Should I stay more than one night?

Most trekkers spend two nights in Samagaun to acclimatise. It’s a great chance to visit Birendra Lake, Pungyen Gompa or head towards Manaslu Base Camp.
