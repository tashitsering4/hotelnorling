Welcome to Samagaun

Life in the shadow
of Manaslu.

Samagaun sits at around 3,500 m on the Manaslu Circuit, in a wide glacial valley beneath Manaslu (8,163 m).

It’s a Tibetan-Buddhist village of stone houses, prayer walls and fields of barley and potatoes — and the perfect place for a rest day before continuing towards Samdo and the Larkya La pass.

Rest-day adventures

Things to do
from our door.

Ask us in the evening and we’ll help you choose the right walk for the weather and how you’re feeling.

Birendra Lake

EasyHalf day

A short walk to a turquoise glacial lake fed by the Manaslu glacier — often with the sound of ice cracking in the distance.

Manaslu Base Camp

ChallengingFull day

A big climb towards base camp (around 4,800 m) for close-up views of the glacier and Manaslu’s summit. Start early and pack warm layers.

Pungyen Gompa

ModerateMost of a day

A beautiful acclimatisation hike to a remote monastery set in high meadows, with sweeping views of the Manaslu massif.

The village & monastery

EasyAny time

Wander the stone lanes, spin the prayer wheels and visit the village gompa — a peaceful way to spend a slow afternoon.

The Manaslu Circuit is a restricted area — trekkers need the proper permits and must walk with a licensed guide. Conditions change with the seasons, so message us for up-to-date trail advice.

Culture & community

Festivals, faith
and a lot of heart.

If your visit lines up with a village festival, you may see masked dances in the courtyard — one of the most memorable sights in the mountains.
