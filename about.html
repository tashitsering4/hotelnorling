A home on the trail

Stone walls.
Warm hearts.

Built from local stone and timber and decorated with hand-painted Tibetan motifs, Norling is a true mountain house. Our name — and our welcome — are rooted in the Buddhist culture of the upper Budhi Gandaki valley.

Over the years we’ve welcomed trekking groups, climbers, families and solo travellers from all over the world. Many arrive as strangers and leave wrapped in a khata, the traditional white or golden scarf of farewell and good fortune.
